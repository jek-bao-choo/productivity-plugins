# Direct Email Template

## Purpose

For drafting a direct email to a colleague, prospect, or customer. The tone should be friendly yet professional, like a warm but efficient coworker or business partner.

## Input Required

- Recipient's name
- Relationship context (colleague, prospect, customer) to calibrate the greeting and tone
- What the user wants to communicate (the intent or key message)

## Email Format

The email must follow this structure:

```
Subject: {Brief, clear subject line}

{Greeting} {Recipient's first name},

{Message body, 1-4 sentences. Get to the point while maintaining warmth.}

{Sign-off},
Jek Bao Choo
```

## Greeting Rules

Choose the greeting based on the relationship and context:
- **"Hi"** for colleagues and familiar contacts (default)
- **"Hello"** for prospects or customers you've met before
- **"Dear"** for formal contexts, first-time introductions, or senior executives

## Tone Rules

- Friendly yet professional
- Short and concise. Respect the reader's time
- No unnecessary pleasantries or filler ("Hope you're doing well", "Just wanted to reach out", "Hope this email finds you well")
- Direct but not blunt. Convey warmth through word choice, not length
- Do not use " — " or " - " to connect clauses. Write short, natural sentences instead
- End with a warm but professional sign-off: "Best regards", "Thanks", "Warm regards", or similar

## Review Checklist

- Subject line is clear and brief
- Greeting matches the relationship context
- Tone is friendly yet professional
- Message is concise (ideally under 4 sentences in the body)
- Intent is clearly communicated
- Sign-off is appropriate
- No sensitive information included unintentionally
